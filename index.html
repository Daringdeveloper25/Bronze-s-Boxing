About Bronze's Boxing

Bronze’s Boxing began with one man’s lifelong passion for the sport. Founder Jonathan Bronze discovered boxing at just six years old and quickly fell in love with the discipline, competition, and determination it demanded. As he grew older, Jonathan competed in numerous boxing events, earning victories and building years of experience in the ring. After retiring from competition, Jonathan wanted to create a place where others could discover that same passion. He founded Bronze’s Boxing as a welcoming gym for fighters of all experience levels—a place to train, improve, challenge yourself, and become part of a community that shares a love for boxing.

Meet the Trainers

Rueben Grinder — Strength & Conditioning Coach

Rueben Grinder is all about building fighters who can go the distance. Known for his intense but encouraging coaching style, Rueben specializes in strength, endurance, footwork, and high-energy conditioning drills designed to keep fighters sharp from the opening bell to the final round. Whether he’s running heavy bag circuits, cardio workouts, or full-body strength sessions, Rueben focuses on helping members become faster, stronger, and harder to wear down.


Specializes in: Strength & Conditioning, Endurance Training, Heavy Bag Workouts, Cardio Boxing, and Footwork Drills.


Henry Powers — Boxing Technique & Power Coach

Henry Powers specializes in helping fighters turn solid fundamentals into sharper, more powerful boxing. With an emphasis on punch mechanics, timing, combinations, defense, and controlled power, Henry works with members to improve both their technique and confidence in the ring. His coaching style is focused and detail-oriented, making him a strong fit for fighters who want to refine their skills and take their training to the next level.




Specializes in: Punch Technique, Power Development, Combination Training, Defensive Skills, and Intermediate/Advanced Boxing.


Edward Throwpunch — Sparring & Fight Preparation Coach

Edward Throwpunch specializes in helping fighters apply their skills under pressure. His training focuses on sparring, movement, timing, ring awareness, and learning how to stay composed during fast-paced exchanges. Edward works closely with members to develop smarter decision-making, stronger defensive instincts, and the confidence needed to perform when the intensity rises.




Specializes in: Sparring, Ring Strategy, Fight Preparation, Defensive Reactions, Timing, and Ring Awareness


Memberships

Copper Membership — The Starting Bell

Designed for beginners and casual members who want access to the fundamentals of boxing and a great workout without committing to the full training experience.

Benefits: Gym access during regular hours, access to heavy bags and boxing equipment, beginner-friendly group classes, basic strength and conditioning areas, and member locker-room access.


Bronze Membership — The Fighter’s Choice

The core Bronze’s Boxing membership for members who want to train consistently, improve their technique, and take advantage of more structured coaching.

Benefits: Everything in Copper, unlimited group boxing classes, heavy bag and conditioning sessions, technique and footwork workshops, open gym access, discounted personal-training sessions, and access to members-only training events.


Champion Membership — Train Without Limits

Built for dedicated fighters and members who want the most complete Bronze’s Boxing experience. Whether you're preparing to compete or simply want to push yourself further, Champion gives you greater access to coaching and advanced training.

Benefits: Everything in Bronze, monthly one-on-one training sessions, advanced boxing and sparring classes, fight-preparation and ring-strategy sessions, priority class registration, personalized training guidance, and discounts on Bronze’s Boxing merchandise and additional coaching.

About this Project

Bronze’s Boxing is a fictional boxing gym website created for educational, portfolio, and career-development purposes. This project was designed to help me strengthen my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive web design, user interface design, branding, and front-end development. The project also serves as a portfolio piece demonstrating my ability to develop an original fictional brand and turn that concept into a complete website. The trainers, memberships, services, imagery, branding, and other content featured throughout the site were created specifically for this project. The concept for Bronze’s Boxing was loosely inspired by the naming style of Gold’s Gym, but Bronze’s Boxing is an independently created fictional brand. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with Gold’s Gym or any other fitness organization. Bronze’s Boxing is not a real business. No memberships, products, or services are being sold, and no profit is being generated from this website. The project exists solely for educational, skill-development, and portfolio purposes.
